Our Story

Sometimes the Grinch doesn't steal Christmas.
Sometimes he saves it.

Fifteen seasons ago, this whole adventure started with a green costume, a crazy idea, and a dad trying to make Christmas happen.

At the time, I was delivering pizzas, working hard to make ends meet, and trying to figure out how my wife at the time and I were going to give my stepson the Christmas he deserved. Money was tight, and the holidays were getting closer. I wanted to make sure there was something special waiting for him on Christmas morning.

So I came up with an idea. What if I became the Grinch?

What started as a five-week side hustle while delivering pizzas turned into something I never could have imagined. I started showing up at homes, surprising families, making kids laugh, entertaining adults, and bringing a little Christmas mischief wherever I went.

By the time that first Christmas arrived, something incredible had happened. That green costume had helped me earn enough money to give my stepson a Christmas.

The character famous for stealing Christmas had actually helped me save ours.

I never forgot that feeling. What started as a little holiday side hustle became a tradition. Every year, the Grinch came back, and every year, more families wanted to be part of the fun.

Over the years, I've surprised children, crashed office parties, interrupted family celebrations, stolen presents as part of elaborate pranks, and even stolen an entire Christmas tree because a family asked me to!

Today, 15 seasons later, what began with one Grinch has grown into a network of holiday performers helping bring Christmas mischief to families and businesses throughout our Illinois service areas.

But through all the laughter, surprises, and holiday chaos, I've never forgotten why this started. It began with a dad trying to make Christmas special for his family. Now we get to help other families make memories they'll talk about for years.

Fifteen seasons later, we're still stealing Christmas.
But the memories we leave behind are the real gift. 💚
